# ChaiTech Business Travel Reimbursement

This fictional policy applies to approved domestic business travel. Business travel requires manager approval before booking. Employees may claim actual meal expenses up to INR 800 per day and actual hotel expenses up to INR 3,000 per night. These are maximum reimbursement limits, not automatic daily payments: a lower actual expense is reimbursed at its actual amount. Itemized receipts are required for meal and hotel claims. Domestic flights should be booked in economy class. Submit receipts and the manager's travel approval to Finance within 30 days after completing the trip. Personal sightseeing, expenses for accompanying family members, and unsupported claims are not covered by this policy. This document does not specify international-travel reimbursement limits.
