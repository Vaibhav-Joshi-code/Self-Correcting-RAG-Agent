# ChaiTech Annual Leave Policy

This is a fictional policy for the Agentic RAG demonstration. ChaiTech employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least seven calendar days before the planned leave. The employee's manager must approve the request before leave begins. Employees should arrange coverage for critical work with their team. This paragraph describes annual leave only; it does not establish sick leave, parental leave, public holidays, or other entitlements. Employees should contact HR if their question falls outside the scope of this document.

## Carry-forward rules

Employees may carry forward up to five unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Those carried-forward days must be used by March 31 of that following year. Any unused carried-forward balance expires after March 31. Carry-forward does not change the manager-approval requirement. This document does not specify a leave encashment policy.
