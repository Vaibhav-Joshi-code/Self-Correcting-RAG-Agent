# ChaiTech Learning and Development Budget

Each ChaiTech employee has an annual learning budget of INR 20,000 per calendar year. This fictional budget covers approved courses, books, and professional certifications related to the employee's work or agreed development plan. Manager approval is required before making a purchase. The employee should submit the purchase receipt and approval record to Finance within 30 days of the purchase to request reimbursement. An employee cannot claim more than the remaining annual learning balance. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the following year. This policy does not promise paid study leave, automatic reimbursement for unapproved purchases, or a separate unlimited certification allowance. The cap applies to the total approved learning expenses for that employee in that calendar year.
