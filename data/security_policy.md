# ChaiTech Lost Device and Security Reporting

If a company laptop or other company-managed device is lost or stolen, report the incident to the IT Security team within one hour of discovering the loss. For this fictional demonstration, the reporting address is it-security@chaitech.example. Include your name, the device identifier if available, the approximate time and place of the loss, and whether sensitive company data may have been accessible. Follow IT Security's instructions on account protection and recovery. Do not wait until the next working day to report a missing device. Employees must not share company passwords or API keys in chat messages, source repositories, videos, or public documents. This policy does not authorize employees to take retaliatory action or access another person's devices.
